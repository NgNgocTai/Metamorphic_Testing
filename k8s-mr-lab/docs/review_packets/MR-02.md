## 2. Phát biểu property đầy đủ

> A RoleBinding grants only the Role named in roleRef from the RoleBinding's namespace; a same-name Role elsewhere grants nothing to its subjects.

Trích dẫn nguồn: [Kubernetes docs, Using RBAC Authorization](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/access-authn-authz/rbac/) "A RoleBinding may reference any Role in the same namespace." (`verified: True`)

Điều kiện giả định: ['RoleBinding to Role only; a RoleBinding to a ClusterRole is out of scope']
Không tương đương đã biết: ['a control that audits every Role legitimately reports the added decoy itself']
