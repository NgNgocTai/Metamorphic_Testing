## 2. Phát biểu property đầy đủ

> A RoleBinding subject {kind ServiceAccount, name N, namespace S} and a subject {kind User, name system:serviceaccount:S:N} grant the bound Role to the same authenticated identity.

Trích dẫn nguồn: [Kubernetes docs, Authenticating (service account tokens)](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/access-authn-authz/authentication/) "Service accounts authenticate with the username system:serviceaccount:(NAMESPACE):(SERVICEACCOUNT), and are assigned to the groups system:serviceaccounts and system:serviceaccounts:(NAMESPACE)." (`verified: True`); [Kubernetes docs, Using RBAC Authorization (Referring to subjects)](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/access-authn-authz/rbac/) "system:serviceaccount: (singular) is the prefix for service account usernames." (`verified: True`)

Điều kiện giả định: ['the subject namespace is explicit; only one subject of one RoleBinding is rewritten']
Không tương đương đã biết: ['a Group subject covers every ServiceAccount of a namespace; a token for a different identity is out of scope']
