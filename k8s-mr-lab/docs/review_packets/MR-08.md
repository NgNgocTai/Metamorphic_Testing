## 2. Phát biểu property đầy đủ

> A RoleBinding subject {kind ServiceAccount, name N, namespace S1} grants the bound Role to ServiceAccount N in namespace S1 only; ServiceAccount N in another namespace S2 is a different identity and receives nothing from that binding.

Trích dẫn nguồn: [Kubernetes docs, Authenticating (service account tokens)](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/access-authn-authz/authentication/) "Service accounts authenticate with the username system:serviceaccount:(NAMESPACE):(SERVICEACCOUNT), and are assigned to the groups system:serviceaccounts and system:serviceaccounts:(NAMESPACE)." (`verified: True`); [Kubernetes docs, Using RBAC Authorization (Referring to subjects)](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/access-authn-authz/rbac/) "the subjects example for a service account lists kind ServiceAccount together with name and namespace (default service account in kube-system)." (`verified: True`); [inference from the two quotes above]() "namespace is part of the identity, hence a binding for S1:N does not grant S2:N (no verbatim sentence states this)" (`verified: False`)

Điều kiện giả định: ['closed bundle; one subject per binding; no other binding', 'equivalent User or SA group reaches the ServiceAccount']
Không tương đương đã biết: ['Role-level controls keep their verdict because the Role is still granted to the moved subject']
